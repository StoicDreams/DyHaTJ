{APP_NAME} works because people pass along real jobs and honest advice. These guidelines apply to everything you share: jobs, tips, comments, profiles, and sponsored listings. Breaking them can get a post removed or an account suspended. They're part of our [Terms](/terms).

## Share Real, Open Jobs

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- Share jobs that are real and still open, with a link to the employer's own posting when you have one.
- Say what you know and how you know it. "My cousin works there" is useful. Made-up details are not.
- Don't share a job that asks applicants to pay a fee, buy a starter kit, cash a check, or send bank or ID details before an offer. Those are scams. Report them instead.
- Don't use the site to recruit for multi-level marketing, or to advertise courses, services, or referral codes.

</webui-page-segment>

## Keep It Fair

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- No job requirements that discriminate by race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, or any other status protected by law.
- No harassment, threats, hate, or personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Don't pretend to be someone else, or to represent a company you don't.

</webui-page-segment>

## Protect Privacy

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- Don't post anyone's phone number, address, email, or other personal details, including your own.
- Don't name or describe coworkers, managers, or interviewers in a way that identifies them.
- Keep your display name to a first name and initial, or a nickname.

</webui-page-segment>

## Give Advice Responsibly

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

- Share what worked for you, and say when something depends on your field or location.
- Tips are community advice. They're not legal, tax, financial, or immigration advice, so don't present them as if they were.
- Don't post pay or benefit numbers you can't back up.

</webui-page-segment>

## Report Problems

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

Use the Report button on any job, tip, comment, or listing that breaks these guidelines. Reports are private. When several people report the same post, it's hidden until a moderator reviews it. Moderators can keep, hide, or remove anything that's reported.

To report a copyright problem or anything urgent, email [support@stoicdreams.com](mailto:support@stoicdreams.com). If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services.

</webui-page-segment>
