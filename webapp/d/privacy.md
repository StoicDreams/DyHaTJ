## Preview Features Keep Your Data in This Browser

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

Sharing jobs and tips, comments, votes, reports, your profile, your application tracker, and sponsored listings are in preview. While they are, everything you add is stored only in this browser (in its IndexedDB storage), and is not sent to {COMPANY_SINGULAR} or anyone else. Other people can't see it, and clearing this site's data in your browser deletes it. The jobs, tips, and people you see in the preview are sample data.

Payment for sponsored listings is simulated. No payment details are asked for or collected.

The Resume Keyword Matcher runs in your browser. The job descriptions and resumes you paste into it are not saved or sent anywhere.

</webui-page-segment>

## What Will Be Public and What Stays Private

<webui-page-segment elevation="10">

When these features move from preview to our servers, this is how your information will be handled. We will update this policy, and its date, before that happens.

- **Public:** your display name and headline, and the jobs, tips, and comments you share, with the date you shared them. Anyone can see them, including search engines.
- **Private:** your application tracker, the posts you mark as helpful, the reports you send, and an employer's billing email. Moderators see reports, and the content they're about, to review them.
- **Not collected:** we don't ask for your resume, and we don't sell the information you add to {APP_NAME}.

</webui-page-segment>
